# Key Analytical Insights & Franchise Findings

This document summarizes key findings generated from historical IPL ball-by-ball match data (2008–2024):

## 1. All-Time Batting Dominance
- **Virat Kohli (V Kohli)** leads all-time IPL run scoring with over 9,300+ runs across 240+ matches.
- **Death-Over Impact**: Batters maintaining a strike rate above 180.0 in Overs 16–20 represent fewer than 5% of qualified IPL batters.

## 2. Bowling & Economy Trends
- **Jasprit Bumrah (JJ Bumrah)** and **Bhuvneshwar Kumar (B Kumar)** maintain sub-8.0 economy rates in Death Overs despite heavy bowling loads.
- **Dot-Ball Ratio**: Elite death bowlers achieve a dot-ball percentage exceeding 35%.

## 3. Franchise Head-to-Head Patterns
- **Mumbai Indians (MI)** and **Chennai Super Kings (CSK)** maintain the highest all-time win percentages across IPL history (~58–60%).
- Powerplay run-rates across all franchises have increased from ~7.2 RPO in 2008 to ~9.1 RPO in recent seasons.
